# credit_card_segmentation
## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing and segmenting credit card customers based on their spending behavior and usage patterns. The objective is to identify distinct customer groups that can help financial institutions improve marketing strategies, customer retention, and credit risk management.

Unsupervised machine learning techniques are applied to group customers with similar financial behaviors.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Credit card companies serve customers with diverse spending and payment habits. Treating all customers uniformly reduces personalization and business effectiveness.

**Goal of this project:**
- Identify different types of credit card users
- Understand spending, payment, and cash advance behavior
- Segment customers into meaningful groups using clustering techniques

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset contains anonymized credit card usage information. Key features include:

- **CUST_ID** – Unique customer identifier  
- **BALANCE** – Current balance  
- **BALANCE_FREQUENCY** – Frequency of balance updates  
- **PURCHASES** – Total purchase amount  
- **ONEOFF_PURCHASES** – One-time purchases  
- **INSTALLMENTS_PURCHASES** – Installment purchases  
- **CASH_ADVANCE** – Cash advance amount  
- **PURCHASES_FREQUENCY** – Purchase frequency  
- **CASH_ADVANCE_FREQUENCY** – Cash advance frequency  
- **PURCHASES_TRX** – Number of purchase transactions  
- **CREDIT_LIMIT** – Credit limit  
- **PAYMENTS** – Total payments made  
- **MINIMUM_PAYMENTS** – Minimum payments  
- **PRC_FULL_PAYMENT** – Percentage of full payments  
- **TENURE** – Customer tenure
